Campus Complaint Management System

An online portal for Sarala Birla University students to submit campus facility, maintenance, and IT issues directly to the administration and track their resolution.

Login to Portal Register as Student

1. Submit a Report

Select the location, category, and priority of the issue. You can attach photo evidence and report anonymously.

2. Track Resolution

Each complaint gets a tracking code. Follow real-time progress from review to work in-progress and completion.

3. Rate & Reopen

Once resolved, rate the resolution quality (1 to 5 stars) or request a reopen if the issue persists.

What Can You Report?

Common campus issues covered under this redressal system:

Classrooms & Labs

Damaged desks, benches, smart boards, laboratory wiring, and computer terminals.

Hostels & Mess

Room repairs, water geysers, dining mess hygiene, laundry, and hostel facilities.

Campus WiFi & IT

Internet access issues, slow bandwidth, portal errors, and lab network connections.

Sanitation & Water

Washroom cleanliness, drinking water RO purifiers, and campus waste disposal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using the portal:

Can I submit a complaint anonymously?

Yes. When filing an issue, check the "Submit as Anonymous Report" option. Your name and roll number will be hidden from staff and technicians, but you will still be able to track the complaint from your dashboard.

How do I check the status of my complaint?

Sign in to your student account and click on any report card. You will see a step-by-step progress tracker, assigned department, and update remarks posted by maintenance supervisors.

What if the issue is not fixed properly?

If an issue is marked resolved but is still not working, open the complaint details and select "Issue persists? Request Reopen". Provide a brief explanation and it will be sent back for review.
