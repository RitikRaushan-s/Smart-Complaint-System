Terms & Conditions of Portal Use

Effective for Academic Session 2026–2027 • Approved by Academic Council

1. Purpose of the Grievance Redressal System

The Sarala Birla University Grievance Portal serves as the official digital gateway for enrolled students to report infrastructural malfunctions, classroom equipment defects, hostel amenity shortcomings, and campus safety concerns directly to authorized university personnel.

2. User Responsibilities & Acceptable Use

By registering and accessing this portal, users agree to:

  • Provide accurate campus locations (block, floor, and room) to facilitate prompt physical inspection by maintenance staff.
  • Refrain from filing frivolous, misleading, defamatory, or abusive reports. Intentional submission of falsified emergency reports is subject to university disciplinary code.
  • Ensure that attached photographic evidence is pertinent solely to the infrastructural or service issue being reported and does not violate individual personal privacy.

3. Priority Classification & Resolution SLA

Reports submitted through the portal are categorized into three service tiers:

  • High Priority: Critical safety risks, live electrical exposures, major water leakages, and total network blackouts. Target initial triage within 4 to 12 hours.
  • Medium Priority: Classroom projector faults, broken desks/chairs, washroom plumbing faults, and air conditioning maintenance. Target resolution within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Low Priority: Cosmetic paint repairs, non-urgent signage updates, and general campus enhancements handled during regular maintenance rounds.

4. Resolution Verification & Reopening Rights

Students retain the right to review the resolution note provided by the department supervisor. If work was not completed to satisfactory standards, the student may request a ticket reopen directly from their overview panel within 7 days of closure, accompanied by an explanatory note.

5. Modifications to Terms

Sarala Birla University reserves the right to amend these guidelines to align with statutory UGC advisories or operational campus updates. Continued use of the portal implies acceptance of any revised procedures.
