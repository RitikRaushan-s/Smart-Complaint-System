Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • In accordance with University Statutes and IT Act guidelines

1. Information This Portal Collects & Stores

The Sarala Birla University Grievance Portal is dedicated exclusively to campus maintenance, facility upkeep, and student welfare. When registering and using this service, the portal securely stores:

  • Student Identification: Full name, student registration number, institutional email address, and cryptographic password hash (bcrypt).
  • Complaint Metadata: Location area, optional building name, floor, room number, grievance category, priority status, detailed issue description, and optional photographic evidence.
  • Operational Logs: Departmental assignments, progress inspection notes posted by supervisors, completion timestamps, and student satisfaction star ratings.

2. Anonymous Reporting Safeguards

When you select the "Submit as Anonymous Report" option:

  • Your name and registration number are cryptographically decoupled from the grievance record in the staff view.
  • Campus administrators, engineers, and maintenance contractors will see the report designated strictly as "Anonymous Student".
  • A secure private ownership link is retained internally solely to enable you to track resolution progress from your personal student dashboard without exposing your identity.

3. How Grievance Data is Used

Information collected is strictly utilized to:

  • Dispatch tickets to responsible engineering wings (Facilities, Electrical, IT, Sanitation, Security, or Hostel).
  • Maintain time-stamped audit trails for university quality assurance and NAAC accreditation compliance.
  • Analyze recurring infrastructural bottlenecks across campus blocks to allocate preventive maintenance resources.

4. Data Protection & Access Control

The portal implements multi-layered security measures, including salted bcrypt password hashing, role-based access control (RBAC) separating student and staff privileges, strict CSRF token validation on all state-changing requests, and secure session management. Grievance records are stored within university-governed database servers and are never shared with external commercial entities.

5. Contact Grievance Officer

For questions regarding privacy, grievance records, or data deletion, contact the University Grievance Redressal Committee at grievance-cell@sbu.ac.in or visit the Dean of Student Welfare office, Academic Block 1.
